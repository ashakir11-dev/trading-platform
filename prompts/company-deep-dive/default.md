# Company Deep Dive: default mode (end-to-end run)

Your brief names one forwarded candidate: the ticker, its direction, and two upstream
folders: the market scanner's and the sector deep dive's.

1. Read the sector deep dive's `companies/<TICKER>.md` (your company's case, score and
   catalysts) and the "Sector view" in its `output.md`. Skip other companies' files.
2. Read the market scanner's market summary and your sector's section, and use its
   `raw/` macro files for the macro backdrop. Do not fetch those again.
3. The catalysts in the company file are the ones you must check, one by one. Add any
   new catalyst or risk you find, marked as your own.
