# Technical Analysis: feedback hints

Look for: stops hit soon after entry (stops too tight for the ATR?), entries that never
filled (entry conditions too far away?), plans sitting at a rule limit that then
failed, targets rarely reached (targets beyond resistance or overhead supply?), and
earnings inside the holding window that decided the outcome. Lessons about level
placement and event risk are useful.

From the chart read: failed breakouts with low relative volume or a distribution
up/down ratio, entries taken extended from SMA50, stage or trend reads that didn't hold,
relative-strength reads that reversed (vs SPY or vs the sector ETF), and whether plans
against their stage, sector or regime (`market_regime` flags) did worse than the rest.
A lesson may tighten a threshold in "How to read the chart" (e.g. the extension limit or
the breakout volume) only when the evaluations show it across runs.
